## section:planned

Places conceived and built to a plan — railway garden suburbs, company towns, planned enclaves — where a single design idea set the street pattern and the housing vocabulary together. Essay to come as the section fills in (Parts 2–6).

## section:historic-core

The old cores: places whose housing predates comprehensive planning and is read through inventories, style catalogues and heritage characterisation studies rather than closed type lists. Essay to come (Parts 7–9).

## section:borough-vernacular

Montréal's borough vernaculars — plexes, shoeboxes and the ordinary housing of the inner boroughs, classified by the boroughs' own typo-morphological studies. Essay to come (Part 10).
