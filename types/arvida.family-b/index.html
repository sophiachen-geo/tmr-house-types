Family B company houses (models B1–B4) Maisons de compagnie de la famille B (modèles B1 à B4)

Photograph pendingpending: no photograph of a documented B model is available under a reusable licence

The second of the 1926 model families: B01 and B02 are scattered through the first streets around parc Oersted, while B03 and B04 appear only later, on rue La Traverse.

Where it comes from

One of the standardised models of the first construction phase, from Brainerd's type plans as reworked on site by Harold R. Wake; see family A for the origin of the 1926 set.

What Ville de Saguenay says about this type

Siting & landscape
  • B01 and B02 run through the 1926 core — rues Berthier, Brittany, Davy, Deville, Edison, Faraday, Hare, Lavoisier, Moissan, Moritz, Oersted and Wohler and boulevard Mellon — interleaved with the A models; B01 also appears once on rue La Traverse.
  • B03 and B04 occur only on rue La Traverse, a later street.
