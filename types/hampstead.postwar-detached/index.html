Photograph pendingpending: photograph on site — rue Thurlow, Vieux-Hampstead (sector 18.E.3)

The houses of Hampstead's second development phase, filling the 1913 street pattern after the war on the same large lots.

Where it comes from

Postwar suburban expansion within the original garden-suburb plan. Bungalows and split-levels are more characteristic of adjacent Côte-Saint-Luc (sector 18.U.2) than of Hampstead itself.
