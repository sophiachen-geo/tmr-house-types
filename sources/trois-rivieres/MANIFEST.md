## 4. Photograph licence procedure

No image was written to `assets/` before its terms had been read. For each candidate the Commons API
was queried with `prop=imageinfo&iiprop=url|extmetadata&iiextmetadatafilter=LicenseShortName|LicenseUrl|Artist|ImageDescription|DateTimeOriginal`
and the returned licence string checked against the allowed set (CC-BY, CC-BY-SA, public domain).
Six of the seven committed files are CC BY-SA 4.0 and one is CC BY-SA 3.0; author, licence and the
Commons file URL appear in the `credit` of every `photos[]` entry that uses them. Candidates found
under `Attribution`-only or GFDL terms, and all RPCQ and municipal imagery, were rejected.

Match confidence is **address-level, not visual**, for every committed photograph: the Commons
category tree under `Category:Site patrimonial de Trois-Rivières` is organised by street address and
those addresses are the same ones the MCC Plan de conservation names in its per-building analysis.
The single exception is flagged in its own credit — `890-terrasse-turcotte-commons.jpg` illustrates
`maison-cubique`, and while the Plan de conservation does record 890 terrasse Turcotte as one of the
site's five hipped-roof dwellings, it does not itself call the house cubique.

## 6. Verbatim French — checked against the pages actually fetched

Both passages the brief quoted were checked word by word against the documents downloaded here.

- **Boomtown roof and ornament.** Matches. "Parfois revêtue de bois, souvent bâtie en brique, la maison Boomtown possède à ses débuts des toitures à faible pente vers l'arrière, dit à égout extérieur, dont la pente peut atteindre 15 degrés." / "Plus tard, avec l'arrivée des membranes étanches, ces maisons adoptent le toit plat, dit à bassin ou à égout intérieur. Étant donné que le toit devient invisible, sa présence est remplacée par des ouvrages ornementaux sous forme de corniche ou de parapet." The brief ran the two paragraphs together; they are two paragraphs on the page and in the fiche. The three bullets quoted after it also match, except that the brief dropped the trailing ", etc." from "platebandes en brique, etc."
- **One small divergence inside the City's own material.** The web page's bullet reads "présence possible de petites fenêtres **de comble**"; the fiche complète PDF reads "petites fenêtres **d'entretoit**". Same for the caption on 634 rue des Ursulines. Both are quoted as they stand in `boomtown.yaml`, and the difference is noted in its `profile_note`.
- **Immeuble à logements.** Matches, but the courant is misnamed in the brief. The sentences quoted — "L'habitation urbaine devient un immeuble à logements superposés et le volume cubique s'affirme sans détour… parapets de brique à gradins et à frises… maisons contiguës, construites en vitesse pour loger les ouvriers" — come from the City's fiche **"Immeuble de type « plex »"**, not from a courant called *immeuble à logements*. The City catalogues that separately from **"Immeuble d'appartements"**, which it distinguishes explicitly: "Contrairement aux bâtiments de type « plex », les immeubles d'appartements possèdent une seule et même entrée commune à tous les logements." The record keeps the ministerial slug and carries both names in `name_fr`; the apartment block with a single common entrance is **not** encoded.
