# Source manifest — Le Plateau-Mont-Royal

Fetched 2026-08-16 per the Part 2 brief §1.1. The 2004 PIIA fiches no
longer exist on the legacy portal (`ville.montreal.qc.ca/pls/portal/...` serves
montreal.ca's 404 shell over HTTP and a plain 404 over HTTPS); they were recovered
from the Internet Archive's Wayback Machine via the `id_` raw-bytes endpoint, with
the snapshot timestamp recorded below. `txt/` holds `pdftotext -layout` extractions
of every PDF; `assets/places/plateau-mont-royal/raw/` holds unreviewed
`pdfimages -j` output from the typologie fiches.

Still to obtain (Constellio GED behind a JavaScript portal): règlement 01-277
Annexe C (consolidated typologies, primary source of record), Annexe B, the two
Annexe A plans, Annexe F (portes et fenêtres par typologie), and the Guide des
travaux extérieurs. Fascicules 1–3 and 6 have no archived snapshots in the CDX
index; only fascicules 4 and 5 were archived.
