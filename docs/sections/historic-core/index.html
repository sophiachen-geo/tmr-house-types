Outaouais · 1800 · 14 types · 1800–2002

GatineauVille de Gatineau

One city assembled in 2002 from five: Wright's timber settlement at the Chaudière that became Hull, the Regency resort and courthouse town of Aylmer, the paper company's Quartier-du-Moulin at Gatineau, and Buckingham and Masson-Angers to the east. Its housing is the Ottawa valley's industrial vernacular — above all the "maison allumette", the gable-front worker's house of the match-works era, built in three generations from the 1870s on the narrow lots of the 1864 Wright survey. In 2023 the City published fourteen type fiches, one per dominant residential form.

Agglomération de Montréal · 1874 · 12 types · 1700–1975

WestmountVille de Westmount

The bourgeois slope suburb of Montréal, grown over the Côte-Saint-Antoine farms between the 1870s and 1930 and controlled almost from the start: an Architectural Commission from 1916, a four-tier grading of every building, and 39 heritage sectors that follow the topography from the greystone rows of the flat lower town up through the plateau and the slope to the summit "châteaux". About 4,750 dwellings, some 61 % of the stock, predate 1945. A National Historic Site since 2011 for "the diversity and the integrity of its components".
