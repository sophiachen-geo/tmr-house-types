Canonical form

Detached modern house — free plan, flat or sculptural roof, ribbon or large glazed openings

The modern movement applied to a house rather than to a block: a free plan throwing off varied volumes, a flat or sculptural roof, wide or ribbon glazing, and materials left expressive in place of ornament. Distinct from the slab and the tower, which are collective buildings.
