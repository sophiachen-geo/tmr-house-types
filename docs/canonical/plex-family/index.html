Canonical form

Plex — the Montréal family of two to six stacked dwellings on a narrow lot

Montréal's defining housing family: two to six dwellings stacked on a narrow lot, each with its own front door, under a flat roof. The Sud-Ouest typo-morphological study defines the family in one line — « Ces immeubles possèdent de deux à six logements » — and the members differ by dwelling count, by whether the stair to the upper doors is inside or outside, and by whether the ground floor is raised.

A family, not a single shape. Its members are pooled from its children: duplex-interior-stair, duplex-exterior-stair, duplex-raised, duplex-three-storey, triplex-interior-stair, triplex-exterior-stair, multiplex-4-6.
