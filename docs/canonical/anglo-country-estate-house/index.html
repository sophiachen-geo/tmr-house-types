Anglo country-estate house — rambling Arts and Crafts manor with ancillary buildings

The large informal country house of an anglophone elite, built on picturesque grounds with stables, lodges and service buildings as part of the composition. Rambling in plan rather than symmetrical, and read together with its landscape rather than as a single object.
