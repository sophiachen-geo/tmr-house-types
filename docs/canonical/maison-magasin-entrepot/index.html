Maison-magasin / warehouse with dwellings above, flat roof, heavy projecting cornice

The mid-nineteenth-century commercial building of a port city, mixing timber, cast iron and stone, with wide bays, a flat roof and a heavy, often richly ornamented projecting cornice, red brick common. Built with dwellings over the shop or store, and widely recycled to lofts from the 1960s onward.
