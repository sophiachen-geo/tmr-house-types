Square in plan under a four-slope pavilion roof whose curved base runs out over a veranda on every side. Six-pane casements arranged symmetrically, gabled or hipped dormers, and clapboard, shingle or brick walls.

Where it comes from

The British officer's cottage as it was built on the south shore facing Québec, and one of the rarest currents in the catalogue at about fifteen buildings. The RPCQ's description of the cited site at Saint-Nicolas names the Regency among the « manifestations d'architecture pittoresque » that give that ensemble its character.

What Ville de Lévis says about this type

Siting & landscape
  • The City counts about 15 buildings of this current across the merged territory of Lévis (fiche, « Nombre à Lévis »).
Massing
  • One and a half or two and a half storeys.
  • Square plan.
  • Hipped (four-slope) pavilion roof with a curved bellcast base, in wood shingle or standing-seam tin.
  • Veranda sheltered by the roof overhang; chimneys.
  • 1 ½ ou 2 ½ étages
  • galerie protégée par débord de toit, cheminées
  • carré
  • à pavillon (4 versants) à base recourbée, bardeau de bois ou tôle à baguettes
Articulation
  • Window and door surrounds, corner boards, entrance doorcase.
  • chambranles, planches cornières, portail d'entrée
Openings
  • Casement windows of six large panes; gabled or hipped dormers; symmetrical arrangement.
  • fenêtres à battants à 6 grands carreaux, lucarnes à pignon ou à croupe, disposition symétrique
Materials
  • Wood clapboard or wood shingle; brick.
  • clin ou bardeau de bois, brique
