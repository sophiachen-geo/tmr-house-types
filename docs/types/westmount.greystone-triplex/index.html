The Montréal plex as Westmount rarely built it: three flats behind a greystone front, the upper two reached by a straight outside stair, on one street at the town's northern edge.

Where it comes from

The 2005 fiche is explicit that this is a borrowed form — « ce genre de construction typique de Montréal se retrouve rarement à Westmount » — which is why it is the cross-link between Westmount and the plex cities of the Plateau and Saint-Lambert.
