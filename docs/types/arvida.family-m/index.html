Photograph pendingpending

The wartime detached house in Arvida's regionalist manner: the French-Canadian farmhouse silhouette re-issued as a company model. M11 (1942) is the textbook example.

Where it comes from

The 1940s phase drew on the regionalist current that the Manoir du Saguenay (Fetherstonhaugh & Durnford, 1939) and later the church of Saint-Jacques (Desgagné & Boileau, 1949) also express; the RPCQ statement singles out houses "davantage dans l'esprit de l'architecture traditionnelle québécoise" alongside those "inspirées de formes populaires aux États-Unis".
