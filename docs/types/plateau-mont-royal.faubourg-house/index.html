Family 1 fiche not retrieved. The typomorphological study describes the villages period (1845–1880) as dominated by the attached single-family house, often built at the street line, with some rear-lot houses (later banned on hygienist grounds) and "quelques maisons typiques de faubourg ouvrier, notamment dans l'ancien secteur du village des Carrières"; survivors on rues de Bullion, Saint-Dominique, avenue Coloniale and rue Lagarde.
