The summit houses — « de véritables châteaux » in the 2005 fiche's phrase — built between 1900 and 1930 for the view, in whichever historicist idiom the owner and architect preferred.

Where it comes from

The summit opened after The Boulevard was linked to Montréal in 1895. Stonehurst and Stonehenge (4200 and 4250 Sherbrooke West, 1928, J. J. Perrault and J. R. Gadbois) are the type's best-known pair.
