Small apartment building Le petit immeuble d'appartements

Photograph pendingpending

A duplex-sized box holding two to four flats off one inside stair; postwar, and rare in the borough.

Where it comes from

Appeared mainly after the Second World War; little used in the Plateau. Post-1945 apartment buildings in general were replacement buildings built one by one across the territory, "une rupture importante avec le paysage urbain existant", often on vacant land along the railway.
