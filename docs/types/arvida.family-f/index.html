The paired company house of the 1940s streets north of the core; eighteen numbered variants.

Where it comes from

The war effort (Arvida the world's largest smelter in 1942) demanded housing fast; the company's own semi-detached models filled Powell, Neilson and Hoopes while Wartime Housing Ltd. put up 500 more prefabricated houses nearby.

Siting & landscape
  • F01–F03 occur singly on 1926-core streets (Deville, Faraday, Moissan, Berthier); F04–F18 occur almost always as pairs ("1727-31 F11", "1738-42 F14") along rues Neilson, Powell, Hoopes, Fickes, Montgomery, Salter, Ohm — the war-era streets.
