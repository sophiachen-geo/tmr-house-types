The fourth 1926 family: five models, two of them (D03, D04) confined to rue Faraday and one (D05) to the later rue Fickes.

Where it comes from

One of the standardised models of the first construction phase, from Brainerd's type plans as reworked on site by Harold R. Wake; see family A for the origin of the 1926 set.
