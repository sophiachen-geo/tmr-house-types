The paired brick house of the early twentieth century, flat-roofed and plain, filling the streets west of the mountain as the summit above filled with châteaux.

Where it comes from

Built as Westmount's middle slope was subdivided between 1906 and 1930; the 2005 fiche contrasts these flat-roofed brick pairs south of The Boulevard with the stone-and-stucco pitched-roof houses to the north.
