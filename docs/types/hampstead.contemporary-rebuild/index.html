Large contemporary rebuilds on the original lots are visible on the ground and documented case by case in council decisions (5851 Ferncroft; 74 Stratford, August 2024; 29 Lyncroft, February 2025; 54 Place Heath), but the Town publishes no demolition or rebuild statistic and no characterisation of the replacement houses. Record kept so the phenomenon is not silently omitted. The four decisions are reported at ingest as evidence of practice, not as a series, and no copy of them is held in sources/hampstead/.
