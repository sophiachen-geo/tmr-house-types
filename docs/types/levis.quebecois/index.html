The house that gives Lévis its street picture: one and a half to two and a half storeys, raised a little off the ground, under a medium-pitched two-slope roof whose curved base carries out over a covered veranda. Clapboard or shingle walls, four- and six-pane casements set symmetrically, gabled dormers, and carpentered trim on the surrounds, corner boards and veranda.

Where it comes from

At about seven hundred buildings this is the second most numerous current in the catalogue, and the one the City's own walking-tour brochure uses to explain how the Norman house of the colonists became something Québécois. The brochure reads the maison Lefrançois at 39 rue Wolfe as exactly that evolution — dormers cut into the roof and clapboard on the walls — and the maison Pampalon at 40 rue Wolfe as its later stage, where the ground floor lifts far enough to make the basement usable.

What Ville de Lévis says about this type

Siting & landscape
  • The City counts about 700 buildings of this current across the merged territory of Lévis (fiche, « Nombre à Lévis »).
  • Documented in Vieux-Lévis on rue Wolfe by the City's walking-tour brochure Le Vieux-Lévis à pied (maison Lefrançois, 39; maison Pampalon, 40).
Massing
  • One and a half to two and a half storeys, raised slightly above the ground.
  • Rectangular plan.
  • Two-slope roof with a curved bellcast base, of medium pitch, in Canadian-pan or standing-seam tin.
  • Covered veranda — sheltered by the bellcast eave or by an independent awning — and a chimney.
  • 1 ½ à 2 ½ étages, légèrement dégagé du sol
  • galerie couverte (par larmier ou auvent indépendant) et cheminée
  • rectangulaire
  • à 2 versants à base recourbée, pente moyenne, en tôle à la canadienne ou à baguettes
Articulation
  • Window and door surrounds and corner boards; a doorcase; veranda trim (knee braces, railings).
  • chambranles et planches cornières, portail, garnitures de galeries (aisseliers, garde-corps)
Openings
  • Casement windows of four and six panes, plentiful and symmetrical; one or more gabled dormers.
  • fenêtres à battants à 4 et 6 carreaux, abondantes et symétriques, lucarne(s) à pignon
Materials
  • Wood clapboard or wood shingle.
  • clin ou bardeau de bois
