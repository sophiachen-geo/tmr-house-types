The house that built Outremont: paired, two storeys, brick, flat-roofed, with its own front door.

Where it comes from

The dominant form of the 1903–1920 build-out. The 2005 evaluation reads the sector's homogeneity as an effect of speed — most of the stock went up inside seventeen years — and of a narrow palette of materials, not of a repeated plan; the houses vary stylistically while the street reads as one thing.

What Ville de Montréal says about this type

Siting & landscape
  • Dominant in the homogeneous north and east, whose buildings went up in a short span between 1903 and 1920.
  • Set on carefully landscaped grounds; the mature trees on the parcels are counted as part of the sector's quality.
Massing
  • Paired under one roofline, each half a single dwelling with its own independent entrance.
Articulation
  • A restricted choice of materials and colours holds the street together, though the houses themselves are stylistically very diverse.
Openings
  • –
Materials
  • Noble materials — stone and brick — characterise the sector.
