Detached single-family house Résidence unifamiliale isolée

Photograph pendingpending: no freely-licensed photograph identified for this type

The detached cousin of the paired house — same brick, same flat roof, standing alone.

Where it comes from

Not a separate tradition but the same house freed from its pair, and the 2005 evaluation treats it that way: a « certain nombre » of them inside sectors otherwise made of semi-detached houses, concentrated around parc Pratt and facing parc Outremont, where the Clercs de Saint-Viateur's 1896 farm-school for deaf children had opened the block.

What Ville de Montréal says about this type

Siting & landscape
  • A minority form inside the homogeneous sectors — « un certain nombre », particularly around parc Pratt.
  • Around parc Outremont it forms a very homogeneous residential frontage facing the park.
Massing
  • Detached, two storeys.
  • Ground floor raised about one metre above ground level.
