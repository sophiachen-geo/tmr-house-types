Two storeys of brick, tall stained-glass windows in twos and threes, and a deep front balcony with carpentry worth describing — either two duplexes built as a pair or one building of three flats with the largest below. It is the form the 2005 evaluation twice calls exceptional, and the only Verdun housing it says that about.

Where it comes from

These are the two residential sectors Verdun's evaluation ranks as exceptional, and they sit in the southern half of the borough, where the cahier says the fabric changes: from avenue Desmarchais southward the space opens up, heights settle at two storeys, exterior stairs become few and the front balcony takes over. That is a genuine typological boundary inside one small borough, and the document draws it in a single sentence. What is protected at Desmarchais and Moffat is not a rare building but an unbroken one: 21.E.3 is judged exceptional « en raison de sa grande uniformité de typologie et d'état de conservation » — the value is in the run, not the unit. Both sectors are inside the territory the 2019– PIIA modernisation brought under review.

What Ville de Montréal says about this type

Description (source)
  • « De chaque côté, on trouve des habitations de deux étages de diverses typologies, notamment des ensembles de deux duplex jumelés ou à trois logements dont le plus vaste est au rez-de-chaussée. L'ensemble présente une belle homogénéité. Le niveau du rez-de-chaussée est sensiblement uniforme sur toute l'avenue. » (21.E.2, l'avenue Desmarchais)
  • « Ce secteur est exceptionnel en raison de sa grande uniformité de typologie et d'état de conservation. Les bâtiments sont, ici aussi, à parement de brique … » (21.E.3, le voisinage Moffat)
Historical context (fiche)
  • « À partir de l'avenue Desmarchais, le cadre bâti prend une composition différentiée des développements précédents. En évoluant vers le sud, l'espace se dégage et la hauteur des bâtiments se généralise à deux étages. Il y a peu d'escaliers extérieurs, mais la présence des balcons avant s'affirme. » (21.I.7, le secteur Woodland et de Verdun)
Siting & landscape
  • Buildings paired two by two along the street; on avenue Desmarchais a broad landscaped median runs between the two frontages and, in the cahier's words, opens the atmosphere out.
  • Ground-floor level is held almost constant along the whole avenue, which is what produces the homogeneity the fiche singles out.
Massing
  • Two storeys. Either two duplexes built as a semi-detached pair, or a single building of three dwellings in which the largest is on the ground floor.
  • Deep balconies across the front. The cahier records no exterior stair here.
Articulation
  • Deep balconies with refined woodwork ("boiseries raffinées") adorning the façades — the phrase is repeated word for word in both sectors.
  • Elaborate cornices and masonry detail (21.I.7, for the same fabric continuing south).
  • « Des balcons profonds aux boiseries raffinées ornent les façades. » (21.E.2, 21.E.3 and 21.I.7 — three times, unchanged)
Openings
  • Tall vertical windows ornamented with stained glass, often combined in groups of two or three.
Materials
  • Brick cladding.
  • « Les bâtiments sont à parement de brique avec de hautes fenêtres verticales et ornées de vitraux, souvent combinées en groupes de deux ou trois. » (21.E.2; the same sentence recurs at 21.E.3 and 21.I.7)

The Évaluation publishes no per-type table, so this record is assembled from sector paragraphs. Its two exceptional-value sectors, 21.E.2 avenue Desmarchais and 21.E.3 le voisinage Moffat, carry one building description between them: 21.E.3 is four lines long and describes the buildings only by back-reference, « ici aussi ». The columns are therefore filled from 21.E.2, checked against 21.E.3, and extended with 21.I.7 (le secteur Woodland et de Verdun), whose fabric the cahier explicitly begins « à partir de l'avenue Desmarchais » and which repeats the balcony sentence verbatim. Dwelling count is deliberately left as the source has it — the fiche allows either two duplexes paired or one building of three dwellings, and gives no way to tell which is commoner — so the canonical link is to the plex family rather than to one of its seven children. Stair position is not stated in either E fiche; 21.I.7 says of the same fabric that exterior stairs are few, and that is recorded as context rather than promoted into the massing column.
