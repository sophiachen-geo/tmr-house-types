Art déco, split-level and International Style, on the same slope and in the same sentence as the revivals.

Where it comes from

Outremont's modern houses have no separate district. The 2005 evaluation puts them inside the mountain sector's list of styles, between the historicist revivals and the picturesque streets — which is a real statement about the place: the flank went on being built through the middle of the century without changing its plan, only its idiom.

What Ville de Montréal says about this type

Siting & landscape
  • The mountain flank, interleaved with the historicist houses rather than set apart from them.
  • Winding streets and varied lot sizes, as for the opulent residences.

Articulation
  • The 2005 evaluation lists them in one breath with the revivals: the sector « présente un échantillonnage de styles évocateurs du passé, mais également des nouvelles tendances du XXe siècle ».
